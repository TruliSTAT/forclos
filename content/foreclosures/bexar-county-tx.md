# Bexar County TX Foreclosure Auction: Listings, Schedule & Bidder's Guide

San Antonio is one of the fastest-growing cities in America, and the Bexar County foreclosure auction runs every single month — making it one of the busiest trustee-sale markets in Texas. Whether you're a first-time buyer hunting a below-market deal or an experienced investor scaling your portfolio, understanding exactly how the **bexar county foreclosure auction** works will save you money, prevent costly mistakes, and get you to the closing table faster.

---

## How Foreclosures Work in Bexar County

Texas is predominantly a **non-judicial foreclosure state**, governed by **Section 51.002 of the Texas Property Code**.

### Mortgage (Trustee) Foreclosures
1. **Notice of Default** — 20-day cure notice to the borrower
2. **Notice of Sale** — 21-day notice posted at the courthouse and filed with the County Clerk
3. **Auction** — Sold to the highest bidder. **No right of redemption** after a non-judicial trustee sale in Texas.

### Tax Foreclosures
Pursued through **judicial foreclosure**, requiring a court judgment. Tax foreclosure properties carry a **redemption period**: two years for homestead/agricultural properties; six months for all other property types.

## Bexar County Foreclosure Auction Schedule

- **Date:** First Tuesday of every month
- **Hours:** 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
- **Exception:** If the first Tuesday falls on January 1st or July 4th, the sale moves to the **first Wednesday**
- **Location:** West side of the **Bexar County Courthouse, 100 Dolorosa, San Antonio, TX 78205**

## How to Find Bexar County Foreclosure Listings

### Official Bexar County Foreclosure Map
The county maintains a live foreclosure map at **maps.bexar.org/foreclosures**, updated twice per month.

### County Clerk's Deed Records
Foreclosure notices are filed as public records:
- **Online:** bexar.tx.publicsearch.us
- **In person:** Bexar County Clerk's Deed Records Department, 101 W. Nueva, Suite B109, San Antonio, TX 78205

### Open Data Portal
The Bexar County GIS Open Data Portal (gis-bexar.opendata.arcgis.com) publishes PDF lists of current-month foreclosure notices.

## Bidding at the Bexar County Foreclosure Auction

### Registration
There is **no pre-registration** for the Bexar County auction. Simply arrive at the courthouse on the first Tuesday.

### What to Bring
- **Government-issued photo ID**
- **Cashier's check or certified funds** payable to the substitute trustee
- **Entity documents** if bidding as an LLC or corporation

### How the Bidding Works
The Bexar County auction is an **open cry sale**. The trustee calls out the property, announces the opening bid, and takes higher offers from the crowd. Payment is due immediately.

### What You're Buying
- No interior inspection prior to auction
- No financing contingencies
- No warranty of title

## Post-Auction Steps

### Title Search (Do This Before You Bid)
Research the title at bexar.tx.publicsearch.us or hire a title company.

### Recording the Deed
After the sale, the substitute trustee prepares and records a **Substitute Trustee's Deed**.

### Dealing with Occupants
In Texas, post-foreclosure eviction follows standard Forcible Detainer procedure through the Justice of the Peace court.
