# Marion County Indiana Foreclosure Auction: Complete Bidder & Homeowner Guide

Whether you're an investor looking to buy property at the Marion County Indiana foreclosure auction or a homeowner trying to understand what happens before the sale, this guide covers the process from default to deed. Marion County — home to Indianapolis — runs one of Indiana's busiest sheriff sale programs, with more than 1,795 foreclosure filings recorded in 2024.

---

## What Is the Marion County Foreclosure Auction (Sheriff's Sale)?

The Marion County foreclosure auction is a court-ordered public sale of real property, commonly called a **sheriff's sale**. This is a **judicial foreclosure** process, meaning every sale goes through the courts. Indiana does not allow non-judicial (trustee sale) foreclosures.

> **Marion County Sheriff Sale is not the same as a tax sale.** Marion County also holds annual tax sales for properties with delinquent property taxes — a completely separate process.

## Marion County Foreclosure Auction Schedule and How to Find Listings

**All Marion County sheriff's sales are now conducted entirely online** through [GovEase.com](https://govease.com).

**Key dates and logistics:**
- **Frequency:** Third Friday of every month (no sale in December)
- **Auction hours:** 10:30 a.m. – 4:00 p.m. (Central Time)
- **Property list available:** 30 days before each sale on GovEase.com
- **Newspaper publication required:** 3 consecutive weeks before any sheriff's sale

**Contact the Marion County Sheriff's Real Estate Division:**
- Address: 695 Justice Way, Indianapolis, IN 46203
- Phone: (317) 327-2459
- Email: MCSO-SheriffSaleRealEstate@indy.gov
- Website: MCSO.INDY.GOV

## How to Register and Bid at the Marion County Auction: Step-by-Step

### Step 1 — Register on GovEase at Least One Week Before

1. Create an account at [GovEase.com](https://liveauctions.govease.com/PublicPortal/PublicRegisterAuctions)
2. Complete a **yearly registration** with Marion County
3. Register for the specific upcoming auction **at least one week in advance**
4. Submit a required deposit

### Step 2 — Research Properties Before Bidding

- **Title search** — Hire a title company or real estate attorney ($200–$500). This is the single most important step.
- **Court case review** — Search the case number on [MyCase.in.gov](https://public.courts.in.gov/mycase/) (use case type "MF" for mortgage foreclosure)
- **Property tax check** — Confirm tax status through the Marion County Treasurer
- **Physical inspection** — Drive by, check assessor records, use Google Maps
- **Occupancy check** — Budget for attorney fees and months of carrying costs if occupied

### Step 3 — Prepare Certified Funds

Marion County requires **certified funds** — cashier's check, cash, or money order. Personal checks and credit cards are not accepted.

### Step 4 — Bidding Day (Third Friday, 10:30 AM–4:00 PM)

Log into GovEase. Properties are auctioned **one at a time in consecutive order**. Pre-bidding is available before the live auction begins.

### Step 5 — After You Win: Sheriff's Deed and Next Steps

1. Receive a **Sheriff's Deed** from the Marion County Sheriff's Department
2. Record the deed at the Marion County Recorder's Office
3. **Get title insurance** — strongly recommended
4. If vacant, you may take possession immediately
5. If occupied, work with a real estate attorney on the eviction process

Indiana provides **no post-sale redemption period**. Once the gavel falls, the sale is final.

## If You're a Homeowner Facing Foreclosure in Marion County

### The Indiana Foreclosure Timeline

From first missed payment to auction day, the full timeline often runs **12 to 18 months** or longer:

1. **120-day federal waiting period**
2. **Complaint filed** in Marion County Circuit or Superior Court
3. **Service and response** — 20 days to respond after being served
4. **Settlement conference** — Indiana law requires the right to request a settlement conference
5. **Foreclosure decree** — court issues foreclosure judgment
6. **3-month waiting period** — before lender can schedule sale
7. **3 weeks newspaper publication**
8. **Auction day** — third Friday of the month

### Your Rights Before the Auction

- **Loan modification** — Contact your lender's loss mitigation department early
- **Forbearance** — Temporary pause or reduction of payments
- **Short sale** — Sell with lender approval
- **Deed in lieu of foreclosure** — Transfer the deed voluntarily
- **HUD-approved housing counseling** — Call 1-800-569-4287

## Tax Sales vs. Sheriff Sales in Marion County — What's the Difference?

| | **Sheriff's Sale (Mortgage Foreclosure)** | **Tax Sale** |
|---|---|---|
| **Cause** | Unpaid mortgage | Delinquent property taxes |
| **Governing law** | Indiana Code §32-29 | Indiana Code §6-1.1 |
| **Administering body** | Marion County Sheriff's Office | Marion County Auditor/Treasurer |
| **Platform** | GovEase.com | Separate tax sale platform |
| **Redemption period** | None (sale is final) | Up to 1 year in most cases |
| **Title conveyed** | Sheriff's Deed | Tax deed (after redemption period expires) |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When does the Marion County foreclosure auction take place?**
Third Friday of every month, except December. Auctions run 10:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. online through GovEase.com.

**Where can I find Marion County sheriff's sale listings?**
All listings posted on GovEase.com approximately 30 days before each sale.

**How do I register to bid?**
Register at GovEase.com at least one week before the sale. Also complete yearly registration with the Marion County Sheriff's Office.

**Is there a redemption period?**
No. Indiana law provides no post-sale right of redemption for mortgage foreclosure sheriff's sales.

**What payment is required?**
Certified funds — cashier's check, cash, or money order. No personal checks or credit cards.

**How long does the foreclosure process take in Indiana?**
Typically 12 to 18 months or longer from first missed payment to auction day.
